# Escalation Playbook

## Principle
The sponsor hears about a problem from me first.

## Steps
Acknowledge the issue the same day. Find the cause with the product or support team. Give the customer a clear next step and a date. Update them on that date even when the fix is not done. Review what happened after the fix and record the lesson in the account plan.

## Product feedback
Bring recurring issues to the product team with account examples so the fix reaches every customer.
