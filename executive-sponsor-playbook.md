# Executive Sponsor Playbook

## Principle
Hard to reach sponsors become reachable when you bring them something useful.

## Method
Go to the daily users first. Learn their goals. Then bring the sponsor a short summary in outcomes and cost language.

## The first ask
Request 15 minutes. Show one result the sponsor's own team produced. Ask one question about what they need to see next year.

## Follow through
Deliver by the date you promised. Make sure the sponsor hears about problems from you first.

## Outcome
Renewal and expansion become planning conversations. I taught this method to the three CSMs I hired and trained.
