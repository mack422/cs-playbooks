# Renewal Playbook

## Principle
A renewal is a planning conversation, not a negotiation. By the time terms are discussed, the customer has already seen the value.

## Account structure
Large accounts involve four groups. A marketing leader is the daily user. An executive owns the budget. An operations or IT group controls integration and security. Procurement and legal appear at contract stage. I manage each group separately and keep one account plan.

## Timeline
At 180 days out I review usage, CSAT and NPS, and open issues. At 120 days I meet the daily users and confirm results. At 90 days I give procurement and legal early notice of timing so paperwork never delays the renewal. At 60 days I bring the sponsor a short summary of results in outcomes and cost language. I put renewal terms and any expansion proposal on the table directly. I stay involved until signature.

## Expansion
Expansion comes from results the customer already sees. I look for cross sells and upsells tied to goals the daily users named.

## Results I tracked
Renewal rate, net dollar retention and expansion share of revenue. My portfolio ran at an 85% renewal rate.
